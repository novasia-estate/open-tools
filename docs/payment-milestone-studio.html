Payment Milestone Studio

Turn dated percentages into an exact schedule. Mark installments paid, review outstanding amounts and export a spreadsheet or calendar reminders.

Your inputs stay in this browser. Nothing is paid, booked or uploaded.

1. Build your scenario

Dates must be ordered, shares must total 100%. Same-day milestones are allowed. Use “paid” only for a fully paid milestone.

2. Review and export

Due today counts as upcoming. Paid status is your own record, not bank verification.

Make the inputs explicit

Normalize a quoted price with the Listing Normalizer SDK, or validate a source table with CSV Quality Lab before preparing a schedule.

Published by NovAsia Estate — property projects and research in Southeast Asia. Examples are synthetic. No exchange rates, taxes, developer terms or contractual dates are inferred.

Precision is your choice, not currency-specific advice. Cumulative rounding preserves the total; a tiny installment can round to zero. No partial payment tracking. Calendar events have no alarms; changed plans may create duplicate identities on reimport. Check contractual dates and amounts separately.
