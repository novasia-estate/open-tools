Your payment schedule

Run the example to see the schedule.
This is a planning tool, not a confirmed payment obligation or legal schedule. Check contractual dates and amounts separately. Events are all-day reminders with no automatic alarm or payment. Each cumulative milestone is rounded to cents so the complete plan sums to the entered total. The same two-decimal precision is used for every currency label; adjust whole-unit currencies in your final agreement.
